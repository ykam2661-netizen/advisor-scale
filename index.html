How it runs

Four steps, in order, every week.

We handle everything upstream of the appointment. You run the conversation.

  1. 01  Find

    We run the ads

    We target retirees and pre-retirees actively looking for retirement income and annuity help — in the states you're licensed in.

  2. 02  Qualify

    We screen every prospect

    Nobody reaches your calendar until the basics are confirmed.

    • $250K+ investable assets
    • Retired or near retirement
    • In a state you're licensed in
    • Wants to talk about annuities
  3. 03  Book

    You speak with qualified people who already know why they're on the call. Every recommendation and suitability call stays yours.

What we commit to

The standard is written down before you sign.

We take one firm per territory, so your appointments are never shown, sold or re-sold to another advisor. Before anything runs, we agree your qualification criteria on a recorded onboarding call — assets, retirement stage, licensed states, and what you actually want to talk about — and that recording is the reference both sides work from.

An appointment that doesn't meet the criteria we agreed isn't an appointment you paid for. We replace it.

Exclusivity is a hard constraint on how many firms we can carry at once. It's the reason the calendar on this page isn't always open.

Every appointment meets all five

  • US resident
  • Retired, or within 5 years of retirement
  • $250K+ in investable assets
  • Lives in a state you're licensed in
  • Actively wants to talk about retirement income

Doesn't meet them? That appointment is replaced free.

Criteria are confirmed with each prospect before booking and are self-reported by them. Replacement applies to advisors who attend the appointments booked for them and follow the agreed follow-up process. The full terms, definitions and remedies of any commitment live in the signed services agreement — we walk you through them on the call, before anything is signed rather than after.
